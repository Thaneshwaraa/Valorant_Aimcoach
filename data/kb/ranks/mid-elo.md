## What actually loses rounds at this level

Mechanics are usually adequate here. Rounds are lost to timing, trade discipline and economy. Players know what utility does but not when to spend it.

## The five fixes with the most value

Trade properly. Standing next to a teammate is not trading; being able to see and shoot the angle that killed them within one second is. Most lost rounds at this level start with an untraded first death.

Fix the round timing. Defaults collapse because everyone commits at a different moment. Call the execute time and hold utility until it.

Stop spending all utility on the entry. Keep one smoke and one molly for the post-plant, which is where most of these rounds are actually decided.

Manage the economy as a team. Full-save together, then full-buy together. Half-buying repeatedly is the single most common economic error in this band.

Learn one map deeply instead of nine shallowly. Knowing every default position, rotation timing and post-plant angle on one map is worth more than surface knowledge everywhere.

## What to practise

Review one loss per session and identify the round where the economy broke. Practise executes with a fixed timing call. Learn three post-plant lineups on your main map for your main agent.

## Getting to the next rank

The jump from this band is about consistency of decisions rather than peaks. Play fewer heroic plays and more rounds where the correct, boring option is taken on time.
