## What actually loses rounds at this level

Rounds are lost to positioning and information, not to aim. The most common causes are peeking one at a time, walking into open space without utility, and everyone rotating on the first sound.

## The five fixes with the most value

Keep the crosshair at head height and pre-aimed at the angle you are about to clear, not at the floor. This single habit wins duels you would otherwise lose before the aim contest even starts.

Stop dry-peeking. Throw something first, or wait for a teammate who can trade you.

Use all of your utility every round. Unused utility is the same as not having bought it. Even a badly placed smoke is better than a smoke you died holding.

Hold one angle and stop repositioning mid-fight. Movement while shooting means you hit nothing.

Buy with the team. One player buying a rifle on a save round loses two rounds instead of one.

## What to practise

Ten minutes of deathmatch focused only on crosshair placement, not on kills. One agent, one role, until the utility is automatic. Watch your own deaths and ask what information you had before you took that fight.

## What not to worry about yet

Complex lineups, agent-specific one-way smokes, and advanced executes. They will not change your win rate at this level. Fundamentals will.
