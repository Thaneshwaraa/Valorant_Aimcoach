## Mid control default

Send two players to Catwalk, one to contest Market, and two to hold A Main and watch for the flank. Smoke Mid Link or Tiles to block the defender angle. Once Catwalk is yours you threaten A Short and B Market at the same time, and the defense has to split its attention.

Do not walk into Mid without a smoke. Open Mid favours defenders, who hold pre-aimed angles from Tiles and Link.

## A site execute

Smoke Heaven and Hell, plus a smoke or wall on CT. Flash over A Main or from Catwalk. Entry through A Main while a second player pinches from A Short. After taking site, plant behind Generator or default near Boxes so post-plant crossfires cover both Heaven and CT.

The most common failure is entering without covering Heaven. If you only have one smoke, put it on Heaven.

## B site execute

Smoke Boathouse, Lane and CT. Flash into B Main. Two players enter through B Main while a third comes from Market to catch the Boathouse rotation. Plant default or near Back Site depending on where your utility can hold.

B is the right target once you have won Mid, because Market lets you enter from two directions at once.

## Fast A hit off spawn

Works when the enemy has been playing Mid aggressively for several rounds. Everyone to A Main, one smoke on Heaven, one flash, entry immediately. Do not run it more than twice in a half; defenders adapt quickly.

## Split pushes

Ascent supports A Main plus Catwalk, and B Main plus Market. Both need a smoke to cut off the rotation and a clear count of who enters first. A split without a timing call is just two separate one-versus-two fights.

## Common attacking mistakes

Entering one at a time instead of together. Spending all utility on entry and having nothing for the post-plant. Planting in the open where every retake angle sees the spike. Losing the Mid player for free and then defaulting anyway.
