## Standard 2-1-2 setup

Two players on A, one in Mid, two on B. The Mid player is the information source and should carry utility to stall rather than to duel. Sentinel setups belong on the site furthest from the rest of the team.

Close the site doors early on rounds where you expect a fast hit. A closed door buys three to four seconds, which is a full rotation.

## A site hold

One player in Heaven, one on site behind Generator or in Hell. Heaven should not take the first duel; it is the trade and the retake angle. Use utility to slow the A Main entry, fall back to Heaven and CT, then retake with the rotation.

## B site hold

One player near Boathouse or Back Site, one holding B Main from CT or Lane. B is small, so playing back and retaking with utility is usually stronger than holding close angles that get flashed out.

## Mid defense

Contesting Catwalk denies attackers the ability to threaten both sites. Do it with a trade partner or behind a trap or turret. Losing the Mid player for free every round costs more than giving up Mid control.

## Retakes

Retake with utility first, bodies second. Smoke the plant, flash from two angles, enter together. Solo retakes on Ascent almost always fail because both sites have long sightlines back toward the spike.

## Rotations

Market is the fast A to B rotation. CT is slower but safer. Only call a rotation before it is confirmed if you still have a Mid player alive to verify.
