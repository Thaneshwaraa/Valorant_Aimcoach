## Layout and identity

Split is a tight, vertical, two-site map defined by narrow chokepoints and elevated Heaven positions above each site. Ropes move players between levels quickly. Mid is the connector, and control of Mid Vents and Mail is what makes fast site hits possible.

## Callouts

A site: A Main, A Lobby, A Ramps, A Rafters, A Screens, A Sewer, A Heaven.
B site: B Main, B Alley, B Garage, B Elbow, B Heaven, B Back.
Mid: Mid Vents, Mid Mail, Mid Bottom, Ropes, Sewer.

## Attacking Split

Split heavily favours defenders because every entrance is narrow and overlooked from above. Attacking without smokes on the Heaven positions is close to impossible at any rank.

Take Mid first. Mid control turns a one-entrance site hit into a two-entrance split, which is the only reliable way to break the crossfires. Budget two smokes for Heaven and one for the site entrance.

## Defending Split

Use the verticality. Playing Heaven with stalling utility below it is very strong. Cypher, Killjoy and Sage are exceptional here because a single wall or trap in a choke can hold a push alone.

A Sage wall in Mid or across a site choke is one of the highest-impact single abilities on this map.

## Common mistakes

Pushing a choke without smoking the Heaven above it. Using the ropes during a fight, which makes noise and removes your ability to shoot. Defenders all playing close angles and getting flashed off site at once.
