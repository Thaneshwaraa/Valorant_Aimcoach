## Layout and identity

Bind has two sites and no middle. Instead it has two one-way teleporters connecting the halves of the map, plus destructible doors on several chokes. With no Mid, rotations are short and fakes are unusually strong.

The teleporter from A Short exits at B Window. The teleporter from B Hookah exits at A Bath. Both make a loud noise when opened and when used, so every teleport gives away information.

## A site callouts

A Short and A Long are the attacker entrances. A Bath and Showers connect from the teleporter. U-Hall links A Short to site. A Lamps and Triple Box are the cover on site. Heaven overlooks from the defender side.

## B site callouts

B Long and B Hookah are the attacker entrances. B Window and B Elbow sit beside the site. B Garden and Back Site are the far positions. Hookah is the strongest attacking position on the map because it offers elevation and cover at once.

## How Bind should be played

Bind is a utility and fake map. With no Mid, information is scarce, so committing all five is risky but very effective when the read is right. Use the teleporters to create pressure on the far site and then rotate back. One player making noise on the opposite site can pull two defenders.

Viper and Brimstone are unusually strong here because the chokes are narrow and hard to clear without smokes. Cypher gets huge value from watching a teleporter, since it is a guaranteed information choke.

## Common mistakes

Teleporting alone into a live site. Ignoring the audio cue that a teleporter opened. Attacking Hookah without smoking Window. Never faking, which lets defenders hold both sites at full strength.
