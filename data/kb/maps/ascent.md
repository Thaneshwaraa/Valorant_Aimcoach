## Layout and identity

Ascent is a two-site map built around a large, open middle. Mid is the pivot: the team that owns Mid can hit either site quickly and can cut rotations. Both sites have a destructible mechanical door that defenders can close, which slows a push and buys rotation time.

Key attacker paths: A Main into A site, Catwalk from Mid into A Short, Mid into Market or Boathouse toward B, and B Main into B site.

## A site callouts

A Main is the wide attacker entrance. A Short, also called Tree, connects from Catwalk. Generator and Boxes are the cover in the open. Heaven overlooks the site from the defender side, Hell sits underneath it. Rafters and CT lead back to defender spawn.

Heaven is the most important angle on A. Any A execute that does not deal with Heaven will get traded from above.

## B site callouts

B Main is the attacker entrance from B Lobby. Market connects Mid to B and is the fastest rotation path. Boathouse and Back Site are the far corners. CT and Lane lead to defender spawn.

B is small and enclosed, which makes it utility-heavy. Smokes and flashes matter more than raw aim here.

## Mid callouts

Catwalk connects Mid to A Short. Top Mid and Mid Cubby sit between the two halves. Market Doors and Boathouse are the B-side exits. Mid Link and Tiles are the defender-side positions.

## How Ascent should be played

Ascent rewards default setups: spread out, take Mid slowly with utility, gather information, then commit late. Fast site hits work only when the enemy has committed elsewhere. On defense, holding Mid aggressively is high value but needs a trade partner, because losing Mid gives attackers access to both sites.
