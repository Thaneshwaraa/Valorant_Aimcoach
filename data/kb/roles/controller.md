## What the role actually is

Controllers decide where the fight happens. Smokes do not hide your team; they remove angles so the fight becomes winnable. A well-placed smoke turns a five-angle site into a two-angle site.

## Core responsibilities

Cut off the angles that make an entrance unwinnable, especially elevated positions. Delay rotations by smoking the connector. Provide the post-plant smoke that makes the retake expensive. On defense, use smokes to stall rather than to block, and to split an attacking push in half.

## What separates ranks

At low ranks controllers smoke the site itself and blind their own team. At mid ranks they smoke the right angles but too early, so the smokes expire during the execute. At high ranks they smoke on a called timing and hold one smoke back for the post-plant.

## The discipline cue

Name the angle you are removing before you throw. If you cannot name it, you are throwing the smoke for comfort rather than for control.

## Agent notes

Omen is flexible and can reposition, which makes him strong on maps with long rotations. Brimstone has the fastest and largest smokes and the best molly for post-plant. Viper controls entire chokes and is strongest on maps with narrow entrances such as Bind and Split. Astra and Harbor trade setup time for coverage. Clove can smoke after dying, which is unique.
