## What the role actually is

Initiators make the entry survivable. They clear the angle before a body walks into it, either by revealing what is there or by making the defender unable to shoot.

## Core responsibilities

Flash or reveal immediately before the duelist enters, not after. Gather information during the default so the team commits to the right site. Clear common post-plant and retake angles. On defense, use recon and stalls to break an execute before it lands.

## What separates ranks

At low ranks initiators flash without telling anyone and blind their own duelist. At mid ranks they flash on time but always from the same spot, so it gets pre-aimed. At high ranks the flash is called, varied, and paired with a body that is already moving.

## The discipline cue

Utility and entry are one action, not two. If your duelist is not moving as the flash pops, the flash was wasted.

## Agent notes

Sova gives the cleanest information with recon and can damage from range. Breach is the strongest raw execute initiator through walls. Skye gives flashes plus healing plus information and is very forgiving. KAY/O suppresses enemy abilities, which is decisive against sentinel and controller setups. Fade gives information plus a debuff. Gekko can recover most of his utility, so he can use it more freely.
