## What the role actually is

Duelists create the first opening. Their job is to take the fight the team needs taken, at the moment the team is ready to trade it. A duelist who gets a kill after the round is already lost has not done the job; a duelist who dies first but opens the site has.

## Core responsibilities

Enter first on executes, with utility already used. Take space in the early round so the controller can smoke into it. Refrag and convert numbers advantages quickly, because duelist kits are built for speed rather than for holding.

## What separates ranks

At low ranks duelists peek before their team is set and die for nothing. At mid ranks they enter on time but use self-sufficient utility only for escape rather than for space. At high ranks they enter on a called timing, use utility to make the entry survivable, and know when not to entry at all.

## The discipline cue

Before you take the duel, ask whether someone can trade you within one second. If nobody can, you are not entering, you are donating.

## Agent notes

Jett and Raze take space and can escape a bad entry. Reyna wins rounds when she is already winning and struggles when she is not, because her kit gives the team nothing. Phoenix and Neon can entry and self-flash. Yoru and Iso reward setup and patience more than raw speed.
