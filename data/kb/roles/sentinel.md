## What the role actually is

Sentinels buy time. On defense they hold a site alone long enough for the rotation to arrive. On attack they watch the flank so the other four can commit without looking behind them.

## Core responsibilities

Anchor the site the team is least able to help. Place utility to delay and inform, not to kill. On attack, hold the flank and lurk to pull defenders. Save one piece of utility for the retake.

## What separates ranks

At low ranks sentinels place all utility at the choke and have nothing left when the site is entered. At mid ranks the setups are good but predictable, repeated in the same spot every round. At high ranks setups change between rounds and are placed to inform the retake rather than only to stop the entry.

## The discipline cue

Your job on a lost site is not to die on it. Fall back, stay alive, and give the retake a second body and a piece of utility.

## Agent notes

Killjoy locks down a site alone better than anyone. Cypher gives the best information and the strongest flank watch. Sage delays a push with a wall and can undo a lost duel with a resurrect. Chamber holds long angles with a strong self-anchor. Deadlock and Vyse deny entries physically.
