## What eDPI is

eDPI is mouse DPI multiplied by in-game sensitivity. 800 DPI at 0.35 sensitivity is 280 eDPI. It is the only number that lets two players compare how fast their aim moves, because 400 DPI at 0.7 feels the same as 800 DPI at 0.35.

## Which eDPI is right

Under 250 eDPI is very low: you may struggle to turn quickly in close fights, so raise it slightly. Between 250 and 600 is the pro sweet spot for Valorant: keep it. Between 600 and 1000 is slightly high: lower it gradually for better precision. Over 1000 is too fast for consistent precision: reset to a proven setting such as 800 DPI at 0.40.

## Changing sensitivity

Pick a setting and keep it for at least 14 days before judging it. Every change resets roughly two weeks of muscle memory, so changing sensitivity after a bad game is the most common way players stay inconsistent.

## Overshooting and undershooting

If you overshoot, flick to about 85 percent of the distance, then finish with a small wrist correction onto the target. If you undershoot, commit fully to the flick and trust your arm instead of stopping halfway. Constant overshooting usually means sensitivity is too high; constant undershooting usually means it is too low.

## Shaky aim and spraying

For shaky aim, practise slow, deliberate tracking on moving bots in the Range. Stop moving completely before every shot, because movement spread ruins accuracy at any range. Tap or burst at distance and only full-spray up close.

## Crosshair placement

Keep the crosshair at head height at all times, not at the floor or chest. Before turning a corner, place it on the top edge of the door or wall where a head will appear, so you only need a tiny adjustment. Placement matters far more than how the crosshair looks.

## Crosshair settings

A clean starting crosshair: Classic style, cyan or green colour, outline on at 0.8 opacity, inner lines length 3 to 4, thickness 1 to 2, offset 2, outer lines off and centre dot off.

## Daily aim routine

Fifteen minutes is enough. Spend five minutes in the Practice Range on medium bots, two on stationary flicks and three on moving bots, one deliberate shot at a time. Then play ten minutes of Deathmatch with one goal: stop moving before every shot and fire only when fully still. Keep this routine for about ten days before adding anything.

## How long improvement takes

In weeks one and two aim can feel worse while new habits fight old reflexes. By weeks three and four crosshair placement becomes automatic. In month two first-bullet accuracy clearly improves. Consistency beats volume every time.
