## Kit

Snake Bite is a damaging chemical molly. Poison Cloud is a re-usable gas orb. Toxic Screen is a long wall placed across the map. Viper's Pit is a large area ultimate. Her fuel drains while abilities are active, and her damage applies decay rather than permanent damage.

## What Viper is for

Viper controls more space than any other agent. The wall can split a map in half, and the ability to re-use the smoke and wall makes her uniquely efficient over a long round.

## Weaknesses

She requires setup and fuel management, and the wall telegraphs the plan. Her utility does not blind, so it delays rather than removes defenders.

## Playing Viper in the lower ranks

Place the wall at the start of the round and leave it up. Use Snake Bite to stop the defuse. Do not run out of fuel before the execute happens.

## Playing Viper in the middle ranks

Manage the fuel deliberately: drop the smoke when you do not need it. Use the wall to cut the site in half rather than to block the entrance entirely.

## Playing Viper in the high ranks

Viper is a post-plant and site-lockdown agent. The pit on a planted spike is close to unretakeable without coordinated utility. On defense, use the wall to stall rather than to deny outright.

## Map notes

Best on large or narrow maps: Bind, Breeze, Icebox, Split, Fracture.
