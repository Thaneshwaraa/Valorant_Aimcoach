## Kit

Leer is an eye that nearsights anyone looking at it. Devour heals after a kill. Dismiss makes her intangible after a kill. Empress is a combat ultimate that boosts fire rate and refreshes on kills.

## What Reyna is for

Reyna converts a won duel into a won round. In the hands of a player who reliably wins the first fight, she snowballs harder than any other agent.

## Weaknesses

Her entire kit requires a kill first. If she loses the opening duel, the team has played the round four-versus-five with no utility. She gives her team nothing.

## Playing Reyna in the lower ranks

This is the hardest agent to climb with, because it rewards the mechanics you do not have yet. Use Leer before you peek rather than after, and take the fight with a teammate behind you.

## Playing Reyna in the middle ranks

Choose between Devour and Dismiss deliberately: heal when you can hold the space, dismiss when you need to reset the fight. Leer should be broken by the enemy, which means it is working.

## Playing Reyna in the high ranks

Reyna is a solo carry pick. Play her only when you are the reliable opener and the team has utility elsewhere. Empress on an eco round can convert a lost economy.

## Map notes

Map independent, because the kit is about duels rather than about space. Best when the team already has a full utility composition.
