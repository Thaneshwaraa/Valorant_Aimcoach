## Kit

Trapwire tethers and reveals an enemy who walks through it. Cyber Cage blocks vision and slows. Spycam is a placeable camera that can dart and reveal. Neural Theft reveals the location of all living enemies from a dead body.

## What Cypher is for

Cypher provides the best information in the game and the strongest flank watch. A single trapwire can hold a flank for an entire round without him being there.

## Weaknesses

His utility is fragile and gives away his setup once found. He has no damage and no escape, so a lost duel is simply a lost duel.

## Playing Cypher in the lower ranks

Place the trapwire deep rather than at the entrance so the enemy walks into it while committed. Use the camera to watch, not to peek with.

## Playing Cypher in the middle ranks

Change trap placements every few rounds. Use the cage to buy time for a retake rather than to block an entrance you cannot hold.

## Playing Cypher in the high ranks

Cypher is an information and map-control agent. Use the ultimate immediately after a trade to convert one kill into a full team read for the retake.

## Map notes

Strong on maps with many flank routes: Haven, Split, Bind, Lotus, Fracture.
