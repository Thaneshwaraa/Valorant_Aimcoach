## Kit

Stim Beacon gives nearby allies a fire rate boost. Incendiary is a lingering fire grenade. Sky Smoke places up to three smokes from a map overlay. Orbital Strike is a large damaging ultimate.

## What Brimstone is for

Brimstone has the fastest and largest smokes in the game, placed instantly from the map. He is the most beginner-friendly controller and the best post-plant molly agent.

## Weaknesses

The smokes are short-lived and cannot be moved once placed, and he has no mobility or escape. The map overlay leaves him vulnerable while he places.

## Playing Brimstone in the lower ranks

Brimstone is the best controller to learn on because the smokes are placed by clicking a map rather than by aiming a projectile. Learn one three-smoke setup per site and use the molly to stop the defuse.

## Playing Brimstone in the middle ranks

Place smokes while behind cover and call the timing before you do. Save the molly for the post-plant every round; it wins more rounds than the entry use does.

## Playing Brimstone in the high ranks

Use Stim Beacon on the execute for the fire-rate advantage in the entry duel. The ultimate is a retake and stall tool as much as a kill tool.

## Map notes

Strong on maps with narrow chokes and clustered sites: Bind, Breeze, Ascent.
