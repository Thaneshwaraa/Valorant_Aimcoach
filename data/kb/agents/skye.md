## Kit

Regrowth heals allies. Trailblazer is a controllable creature that concusses. Guiding Light is a steerable flash hawk that signals when it hits someone. Seekers is an ultimate that tracks enemies.

## What Skye is for

Skye is the most forgiving initiator: her flashes are steerable, tell her when they hit, and can be used repeatedly. She also heals and gathers information.

## Weaknesses

Her flashes are slower than instant flashes and can be dodged by an aware enemy. The healing does not affect herself.

## Playing Skye in the lower ranks

Skye is the best initiator to learn on. Bend the flash around the corner and enter immediately when it signals a hit. Heal teammates between rounds rather than mid-fight.

## Playing Skye in the middle ranks

Use the flash from a position where you can also trade. Trailblazer is an information and stall tool, not a kill tool.

## Playing Skye in the high ranks

Skye is an information and re-flash agent. Use the hit indicator to call whether the site is occupied before the team commits.

## Map notes

Strong on nearly every map. Especially good on maps with corners to bend flashes around, such as Bind and Split.
