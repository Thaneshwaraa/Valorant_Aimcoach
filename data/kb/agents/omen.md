## Kit

Shrouded Step is a short teleport. Paranoia is a nearsight projectile that travels through walls. Dark Cover places smokes that can be thrown far and re-placed. From the Shadows teleports him anywhere on the map.

## What Omen is for

Omen is the most flexible controller. He can smoke from safety, reposition without walking, and threaten a position on the far side of the map. Paranoia is one of the strongest execute tools in the game.

## Weaknesses

His smokes are smaller and shorter than Brimstone's, and the teleport is loud and visible on arrival. He needs setup time to place smokes well.

## Playing Omen in the lower ranks

Learn two smoke placements per site and use them every round. Do not teleport into site for value; use Shrouded Step to take an angle safely. Paranoia before an entry, not after.

## Playing Omen in the middle ranks

Re-place a smoke mid-round to change the fight rather than throwing both at the start. Use the teleport to fake a rotation or to take Mid without walking through it.

## Playing Omen in the high ranks

Omen is a map-control and information denial agent. Use the ultimate to reposition for a lurk or to break a stacked read, and hold one smoke for the post-plant.

## Map notes

Excellent everywhere. Especially strong on maps with long rotations such as Haven and Lotus, where the teleport saves real time.
