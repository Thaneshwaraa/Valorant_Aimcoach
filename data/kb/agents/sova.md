## Kit

Owl Drone is a controllable scouting drone that can tag enemies. Shock Bolt is a damaging arrow. Recon Bolt reveals enemies in an area and can bounce. Hunter's Fury fires damaging energy blasts through walls.

## What Sova is for

Sova gives the cleanest information in the game and can convert that information into damage from complete safety. A single recon bolt can decide whether a site is worth attacking.

## Weaknesses

His value depends on learned lineups, and the drone and bolts are loud and easy to shoot down. Without map knowledge his kit is far weaker than it looks.

## Playing Sova in the lower ranks

Learn two recon lineups per site and use them before the execute, not during it. Shock bolts are for stopping the defuse. Do not drone into site and then walk in behind it with no information gained.

## Playing Sova in the middle ranks

Vary the recon position so it does not get pre-shot. Use the drone during a default to confirm the site is empty before committing five players.

## Playing Sova in the high ranks

Sova is an information and post-plant agent. Use the ultimate to clear a known position or to force a rotation rather than hoping for a blind kill.

## Map notes

Strongest on open maps with bounce surfaces: Ascent, Breeze, Icebox, Haven.
