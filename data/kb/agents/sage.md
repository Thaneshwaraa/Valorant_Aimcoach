## Kit

Barrier Orb creates a solid wall. Slow Orb creates a slowing field. Healing Orb heals an ally or herself. Resurrection revives a dead teammate.

## What Sage is for

Sage buys time better than any other agent. The wall can stop an execute outright, and the resurrect can undo the single most important death of a round.

## Weaknesses

The wall can be broken and telegraphs the plan. Her kit does no damage and provides no information, so she depends on teammates to convert the time she buys.

## Playing Sage in the lower ranks

Wall the entrance to your site when you hear the push, not at the start of the round. Heal teammates who are still in the fight rather than healing yourself in safety.

## Playing Sage in the middle ranks

Use the wall to take space on attack, not only to defend. Slow orb the choke behind an entry to stop the trade. Resurrect the player whose utility the round still needs.

## Playing Sage in the high ranks

Sage is a tempo and stall agent. Wall placement that splits a site in half is more valuable than a wall that blocks an entrance. Save the resurrect for a player, not a moment.

## Map notes

Best on maps with narrow chokes she can wall: Split, Bind, Icebox, Ascent B.
