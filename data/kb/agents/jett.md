## Kit

Cloudburst is a short self-smoke. Updraft launches her upward. Tailwind is a dash that must be activated first and then used within a short window. Blade Storm is her ultimate, a set of throwing knives that refresh on kills.

## What Jett is for

Jett takes space that no other agent can take and gets out of positions no other agent survives. She is the strongest Operator holder in the game because the dash lets her take a shot and leave before the trade arrives.

## Weaknesses

Her utility gives the team almost nothing. If the dash is used to enter, she has no escape; if it is saved for escape, she is not entering. She is punished hard by suppression and by pre-aimed angles she cannot dash away from.

## Playing Jett in the lower ranks

Do not dash into site as an opening move. Dash to reposition after taking a duel, and use the smoke to cross open space rather than to hide on site. Most Jett deaths at this level are a dash into three players.

## Playing Jett in the middle ranks

Activate the dash before you peek so it is available, and treat it as your escape rather than your entry. Use Updraft to take an unexpected angle once per round, not repeatedly from the same spot.

## Playing Jett in the high ranks

Jett is an Operator and tempo agent. The dash creates timings the enemy cannot pre-aim. Use knives on eco rounds to convert an economic advantage without spending credits.

## Map notes

Strongest on open maps with long angles: Ascent, Breeze, Icebox. Weaker on tight maps such as Split where the dash has nowhere to go.
