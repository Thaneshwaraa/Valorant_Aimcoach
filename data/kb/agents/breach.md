## Kit

Aftershock is a damaging blast through a wall. Flashpoint is a flash that fires through a wall. Fault Line is a stun that travels through terrain. Rolling Thunder is a large stunning ultimate.

## What Breach is for

Breach is the strongest raw execute initiator because all of his utility passes through walls, so he can clear a site without ever exposing himself.

## Weaknesses

He has no information gathering and no escape. His flashes blind teammates easily and require coordination to use well.

## Playing Breach in the lower ranks

Face the wall, aim at the site, and use the utility before your team enters. The most common mistake is flashing after the duelist has already peeked.

## Playing Breach in the middle ranks

Pair the stun with the entry timing rather than throwing on a fixed clock. Aftershock the common close angle rather than the open site.

## Playing Breach in the high ranks

Breach is a coordinated execute and retake agent. The ultimate through a site is one of the best retake tools in the game when paired with entries.

## Map notes

Best on maps with narrow entrances and predictable defender positions: Split, Bind, Lotus, Haven.
