## Kit

Trademark is a trap that slows enemies in an area. Headhunter is a purchasable pistol with high damage. Rendezvous places an anchor he can teleport back to. Tour de Force is a sniper rifle ultimate that kills on body shots.

## What Chamber is for

Chamber holds long angles better than any other sentinel because the teleport lets him take a shot and leave. He is an economy agent as much as a defensive one.

## Weaknesses

His trap coverage is far weaker than Killjoy's or Cypher's, so he anchors a site less well than his role suggests. He depends on aim more than on utility.

## Playing Chamber in the lower ranks

Play Chamber as an angle holder rather than as a site anchor. Set the teleport anchor before you peek so the escape exists.

## Playing Chamber in the middle ranks

Use Headhunter on eco rounds to keep the economy healthy. Place the anchor to retreat toward the rotation, not toward the corner.

## Playing Chamber in the high ranks

Chamber is a tempo and economy agent. The ultimate holds a long angle for free, which is most valuable on a save or a force round.

## Map notes

Best on maps with long sightlines: Breeze, Ascent, Icebox, Haven.
