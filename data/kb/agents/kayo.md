## Kit

FRAG/ment is a damaging explosive that sticks to the floor. FLASH/drive is a throwable flash that can be curved by holding the fire button. ZERO/point is a suppression knife that disables enemy abilities in an area. NULL/cmd suppresses a large area and lets him be revived after death.

## What KAY/O is for

KAY/O is the only agent who removes enemy abilities. Suppression turns a locked-down site into an ordinary one and is decisive against sentinel and controller setups.

## Weaknesses

His information is limited to what the knife reveals, and his flashes are shorter than most. He depends on the team converting the suppression window.

## Playing KAY/O in the lower ranks

Use the flash by holding fire for a quick curve, and enter as it pops. Throw the knife at the site before the execute rather than at a player.

## Playing KAY/O in the middle ranks

Time the knife so the suppression window covers the entry, not the approach. FRAG/ment is a strong post-plant denial tool.

## Playing KAY/O in the high ranks

KAY/O is a counter-utility agent. Save the ultimate for rounds where enemy utility, rather than enemy aim, is what beat you.

## Map notes

Strong everywhere, and specifically valuable against Killjoy, Cypher, Sage and Viper setups on Split, Bind and Icebox.
