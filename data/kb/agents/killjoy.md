## Kit

Nanoswarm is a deployable grenade that becomes a damaging swarm. Alarmbot detects and makes enemies vulnerable. Turret watches an angle and reveals. Lockdown is an ultimate that detains everyone in a large radius.

## What Killjoy is for

Killjoy holds a site alone better than any other agent. Her utility informs, damages and delays at once, which is exactly what an anchor needs.

## Weaknesses

Her utility has a range limit from her own position and can be destroyed. She is weak against KAY/O suppression and against coordinated utility clearing.

## Playing Killjoy in the lower ranks

Place the turret to watch the entrance and the alarmbot deeper, so you learn about the push and then about the entry. Do not stand on top of your own utility.

## Playing Killjoy in the middle ranks

Vary placements between rounds. Keep one nanoswarm for the post-plant defuse denial rather than spending both on the entry.

## Playing Killjoy in the high ranks

Killjoy is a site-lockdown and post-plant agent. Lockdown is a retake and a stall tool; use it to break a plant rather than to defend an empty site.

## Map notes

Best on maps with enclosed sites: Ascent, Split, Bind, Icebox, Haven.
