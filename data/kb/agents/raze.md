## Kit

Boom Bot is a scouting drone that chases and damages. Blast Pack is a satchel that damages and launches her, and she carries two. Paint Shells is a cluster grenade. Showstopper is a rocket launcher ultimate.

## What Raze is for

Raze clears tight spaces with damage rather than with aim. She is the best agent in the game at forcing players off a close angle and at punishing clustered defenders.

## Weaknesses

She is loud, so she cannot lurk or take a quiet flank. Her utility does not work on open maps with long sightlines, and Blast Pack movement is hard to control under pressure.

## Playing Raze in the lower ranks

Use Boom Bot down a corridor before entering rather than throwing it randomly. Practise a single satchel jump into your main site until it is reliable. Do not hold Paint Shells until you die.

## Playing Raze in the middle ranks

Chain the satchels: one to reposition, one to escape. Use the grenade to deny the post-plant angle rather than only for entry. Boom Bot on defense is an excellent stall.

## Playing Raze in the high ranks

Raze is a tempo and space agent. Use double satchel to take an angle the enemy cannot pre-aim, and save the ultimate for a stacked site or a retake rather than for a single kill.

## Map notes

Strongest on tight maps: Split, Bind, Fracture, Lotus. Weakest on open maps such as Breeze and Ascent Mid.
