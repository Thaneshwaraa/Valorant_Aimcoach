## Why post-plant decides rounds

Most rounds at every rank are lost after the spike is down, not before. The attacking team takes the site, plants, then crowds the spike and loses to a coordinated retake.

## Holding the plant

Spread out and hold different angles. Two players watching the same entrance means the other entrance is free.

Do not stand where you can see the spike, because that is exactly where the retake expects you. Hold the angle that punishes someone walking to the spike instead.

Play for the clock. You do not need to win the fight; you need the timer to run out. Time wasted is time won.

## Utility

Keep one molly or one damaging ability for the defuse. This is the single highest-value habit in the whole game and is skipped constantly.

Smoke the spike when the retake begins. It denies the defenders sightlines onto your held angles and forces them into close-range fights.

Watch the defuse sound and the half-defuse. A defuse that stops halfway means the enemy will finish it in half the time later, so treat the second attempt as much shorter.

## Rank-specific guidance

In the lower ranks, simply keeping one molly for the defuse is worth several rounds per game. In the middle ranks, spreading the post-plant angles is the gain. In the high ranks, post-plant is about denying the specific defuse position and about clock management.
