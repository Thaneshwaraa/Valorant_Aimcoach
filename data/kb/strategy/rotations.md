## What a rotation actually costs

A rotation is not free. It costs the time to walk, the information the enemy gains from your absence, and the site you left. Rotating on the first sound is the most common way to lose a site without a fight.

## When to rotate

Rotate on confirmed information: a spike plant, a confirmed player count, or a teammate death that proves where the enemy is. Do not rotate on a single footstep or on one utility sound, both of which are cheap for the enemy to fake.

Leave someone behind. A full rotation opens the site you left. One player staying is the difference between a fake working and a fake being punished.

## Attacking rotations

On attack, the goal is to make the defense rotate and then punish the rotation. A fake needs three things to be believed: utility spent, noise made, and a plausible number of players. A single player walking loudly into A main does not convince anyone above the lowest ranks.

Rotate through the fastest connector only when you have the map control to use it safely. On Ascent that is Market, on Haven it is Garage and the links, on Lotus it is the site links.

## Defensive rotations

Call the rotation, do not assume it. Two players rotating from opposite sites means both sites are now empty.

Rotate with utility ready. Arriving at a taken site with no smoke and no flash means arriving to lose a retake.

## Rank-specific guidance

In the lower ranks, the fix is simply to stop over-rotating on sound. In the middle ranks, the fix is to rotate late and with utility rather than early and empty. In the high ranks, rotation timing is a tool: rotating visibly to bait a re-rotation is a legitimate play.
