## The basic rule

Buy as a team or save as a team. A single player buying a rifle on a save round converts one lost round into two lost rounds, because the team is then split between armed and unarmed players.

## Round types

Full buy means rifle, full shields and utility for everyone. This is the only round type you should expect to win consistently.

Full save, sometimes called an eco, means pistols only and no shields, keeping the credits so the next round is a full buy. Play these rounds for damage and for stealing weapons, not for the win.

Half buy or force buy means light shields plus a cheap weapon. Force only when winning the round matters more than the next one: to break a losing streak bonus, to deny the enemy a comfortable buy, or at match point.

Bonus round means the enemy is on a save and you are buying anyway. Do not over-invest; buy enough to win comfortably.

## Practical guidance

Keep at least the price of a rifle plus shields in reserve when you can. If the team cannot all afford a full buy, everyone saves.

Requesting and dropping weapons matters. A player with credits should drop for a player without, because five armed players beat four armed players and one rich one.

Winning a save round is worth less than it feels. It often puts you into a broken economy for the next two rounds because you spent nothing and gained little.

## What goes wrong at each level

In the lower ranks, players buy independently every round and the team never has a coordinated full buy. In the middle ranks, the most common error is repeated half-buying, which keeps the team permanently under-equipped. In the high ranks, economy mistakes are usually about failing to force at the right moment rather than about overspending.
