## The anatomy of an execute

Every working execute has four parts in the same order: smokes to remove the angles that cannot be cleared, a flash or reveal immediately before entry, bodies entering together, and utility held back for the post-plant.

If any of the four is missing, the execute is a hope rather than a plan.

## Timing

The execute begins on a called count, not when the first player feels ready. The most common failure is smokes going up several seconds before anyone moves, so the defenders reposition and the smokes expire.

Smokes, then flash, then entry, all within about two seconds.

## Entry order

The duelist enters first, the initiator supports and follows immediately to trade, the controller holds the smoke line, and the sentinel watches the flank. Entering one at a time turns a five-versus-five into five one-versus-fives.

## The plant

Plant where your utility can hold, not in the open middle of the site. A default plant that your team can watch from two angles is worth more than a fast plant nobody can cover.

Announce the plant position so the team takes post-plant angles rather than crowding the spike.

## After the plant

Do not all watch the same entrance. Spread the angles, hold the crossfire, and use the held-back molly or smoke to deny the defuse rather than to kill.

## Rank-specific guidance

In the lower ranks, simply entering together and planting in a covered spot is worth more than any specific utility line. In the middle ranks, the gain comes from holding utility for the post-plant. In the high ranks, executes should vary in timing and in entrance so they cannot be pre-read.
