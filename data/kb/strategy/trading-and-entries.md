## What a trade actually is

A trade is killing the enemy who killed your teammate, before they can reset. Standing near a teammate is not trading. Being able to see and shoot the exact angle that killed them, within about one second, is trading.

If you cannot see the angle, you are not the trade; you are the next death.

## Entry discipline

Before taking a duel, answer three questions. Is my utility used? Can someone trade me? Does winning this duel gain the team space it needs?

If the answer to any is no, the duel is optional, and optional duels lost at a bad time lose rounds.

## Spacing

Too close and one flash or one grenade catches both players. Too far and the trade arrives too late. The right distance is close enough to see the same angle and far enough that one piece of utility cannot hit both.

## Peeking

Wide peeks beat players holding a tight angle. Shoulder peeks gather information for free. Dry peeking a known angle without utility is the single most common avoidable death in the game.

Jiggle for information, commit only when you have a reason.

## Rank-specific guidance

In the lower ranks, the fix is to stop peeking one at a time and to pre-aim at head height. In the middle ranks, the fix is trade positioning: most lost rounds start with an untraded first death. In the high ranks, entries are about timing and about denying the enemy the trade, rather than about winning the first duel outright.
